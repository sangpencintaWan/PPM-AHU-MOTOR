AHU Motor – Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM)

Air Handling Unit motor, fan and drive inspection checklist

1. Equipment details

2. Safety & isolation

1Notify building owner / occupants of shutdown
2Isolate power, apply lock-out / tag-out (LOTO)
3Verify zero energy with tester before touching terminals
4PPE worn (gloves, safety shoes, eye and ear protection)

3. Mechanical inspection

5Motor body clean, vents and cooling fins free of dust
6Motor mounting bolts and base frame tight, no cracks
7Anti-vibration mounts / isolators in good condition
8Bearings: no abnormal noise, heat or grease leakage
9Grease bearings (correct type and quantity per nameplate)
10Shaft free to rotate by hand, no rubbing or play
11Fan wheel / blades clean, balanced, no damage
12Fan shaft bearings and plummer blocks lubricated

4. Drive system (belt & pulley)

13V-belts: no cracks, glazing or fraying; replace as a matched set
14Belt tension correct (about 10–15 mm deflection, per manufacturer)
15Pulley alignment checked, grooves not worn
16Pulley set screws / taper lock bushes tight
17Belt guard secure and undamaged

5. Electrical inspection

18Terminal box clean and dry, no moisture or burn marks
19Terminal connections tight, no discolouration
20Cable glands, cable insulation and earthing in good condition
21Starter / VSD / contactor and overload relay setting checked
22Starter panel clean, indicators and push buttons working
23Motor rotation direction correct
